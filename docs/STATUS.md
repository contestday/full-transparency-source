## Known limitations and decisions

- **shadcn/ui:** primitives are hand-written in the same style (`src/components/ui.tsx`) rather than generated by the shadcn CLI. Behaviour is equivalent and there are no extra dependencies.
- **Venue lookup** searches saved venues only. Locations are set by tapping the map, with no address geocoding (which would send venue searches to a third party).
- **Area labels** come from admin-defined area rules or the venue's label. There is no automatic reverse geocoding.
- **Incident time** on reports is stored as entered, treated as UTC and flagged approximate. During British Summer Time it reads one hour off. Fix it before relying on report times.
- **Publication pause** holds the backlog. On resume, records from the paused period become eligible. To keep a period permanently private, withdraw it (Settings → Withdraw) after resuming.
- **Public geofence display** is not supported (spec 8.2 requires separate moderation).
- The scaffold's experimental `cacheComponents`/`partialPrefetching` flags were turned off. Pages use standard dynamic rendering.
- The original asset pack is kept in `full-transparency-assets/` for provenance. The live copies are in `public/full-transparency/` and `src/app/`.
